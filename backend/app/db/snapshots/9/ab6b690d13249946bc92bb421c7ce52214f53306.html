Key Features

Smart Features, Real Impact

Explore intelligent tools designed to boost efficiency, enhance engagement, and deliver measurable business growth.
Real-Time Monitor, Whisper, Spy, and Take Over
Experience a world-class innovation — we are the only company globally that allows you to monitor AI and human agents simultaneously, perform real-time sentiment and frustration analysis, and instantly decide whether to debrief, observe, whisper to the AI or customer directly, or even take full control of the conversation!
AI Agent – No-Code Configuration
Experience a true no-code solution with full API integration, empowering you to design dialogs, create workflows, and connect with external systems seamlessly. Leverage your knowledge database and business intelligence without limitations by defining dynamic KPIs and business goals directly within each dialog — and monitor their achievement in every conversation!
Dial Plan Management
The Dial Plan enables seamless voice connectivity between users and the AI Agent through SIP integration. From the Dial Plan Manager, users can configure and manage SIP settings to allow direct calling and smooth communication with the AI Agent.
Call Logs Analytics
The Call Log provides a comprehensive view of all past interactions between customers and the AI Agent. It includes detailed call information such as duration, language, and campaign data, along with call recordings, conversation summaries, and sentiment analysis results.
Experience the unique ability to extract real data value from conversations — seamlessly integrated with your backend systems.
Our solution goes beyond simple data collection: it enables you to define and measure your own KPIs directly from live interactions. Set your business goals, track customer interest levels, or identify specific answers and outcomes — and instantly receive automated reports generated through real-time conversation monitoring, giving you the insights that truly matter.
Support Tickets
The Support Ticket system allows users to easily raise tickets for any issues or concerns they encounter. Once submitted, our Service Excellence team reviews the ticket and provides timely assistance with the best possible solution, ensuring smooth operation and continued user satisfaction.
