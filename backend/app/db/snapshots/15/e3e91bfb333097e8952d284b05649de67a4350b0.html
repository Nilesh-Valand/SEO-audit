Frequently asked questions

Our marketing automation tools help you convert more customers and deliver a great experience at every step, from signing someone up for your newsletter to sending them great emails and SMS messages. They help you make the most of everything that happens after a customer gets to your store, and they go hand in hand with Shopify's advertising and traffic-generating tools, like sales channels, SEO, and more. Marketing automation tools include Shopify Messaging, Shopify Inbox, segmentation, and marketing automations.

To learn more about the types of marketing automation tools, check out the 48 best marketing tools for every business on Shopify's blog.
New marketers can get their marketing efforts up and running in no time (and usually at no cost) with easy-to-use, preset templates designed to quickly get businesses in front of new customers. Established marketers love how these tools grow with their business, pulling in their store branding to marketing materials and helping them create more personalized marketing campaigns based on easy-to-access customer data.
Shopify has tools to help you generate traffic to your store, so you can deliver a great marketing experience once they're there. These include:
  • Shopify Collabs
  • Shopify Audiences
  • Sales channels for Facebook, Instagram, Tiktok, and YouTube
  • Online marketplaces for Google and Walmart
  • Built-in content marketing automation tools in the admin, such as blog posts
  • Built-in SEO tools in the admin, such as auto canonical tags; auto sitemap.xml and robots.xml; Editor for title tags, meta descriptions, URLs (blog posts, webpages, products, collections); and editor for image alt text.
Shopify Inbox, Shopify Forms, automations, and segmentation are all 100% free to all businesses on Shopify's Basic plan and above. The only product that comes with a fee is Shopify Messaging, and that fee only begins after you start sending over 10,000 emails per month. SMS marketing is charged separately.
