Climate Entrepreneurs

Shopify Sustainability Fund

The Shopify Sustainability Fund supports entrepreneurs developing breakthrough climate technologies. As part of this, we are a founding member of Frontier, an advance market commitment to purchase an initial $1 billion of permanent carbon removal by 2030.

We back groundbreaking technologies that are removing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere or preventing its release in the first place.

Carbon-rich waste that's injected deep underground, fans sponging the air of greenhouse gasses, devices that capture emissions from semi-trucks as they drive – no solution is too ambitious or too crazy.

Our goal is to help prove, scale, and commercialize climate technology for massive impact in the long term. Because at the end of the day, commerce can only thrive if our planet thrives too.

Shopify merchants and app developers

Shopify Planet

Shopify merchants can use Planet to offer carbon-neutral shipping, helping them appeal to modern consumers, all while supporting the same trailblazing projects that Shopify backs.

Planet works by estimating shipping emissions, and enabling both merchants and their customers to neutralize the carbon produced by their shipments. All of the money that Planet generates for carbon removal goes to entrepreneurial, early-stage companies that are actually removing emissions from the atmosphere.

With Planet, commerce entrepreneurs are funding climate entrepreneurs, and it’s happening via the entrepreneurship company – Shopify.

Sell Credits On Shopify

Carbon Commerce

Climate companies can sell and manage carbon credits with Shopify – the all-in-one commerce platform for everything from multi-year corporate offtakes to monthly subscriptions.

With Shopify, companies can transact every type of deal, have their own storefront, confidently manage inventory, integrate with carbon removal registries, and more. Shopify positions companies to handle growth, mix-and-match tools, automate processes, and puts them at the bleeding edge.
